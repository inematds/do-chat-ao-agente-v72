The lessons

An overview of each step. Each step has a full course, linked at the end of its lesson.

  1. Lesson 1 · 6 minFrom chat to agent: the four stepsIn chat, you guide everything, and when you close the window, the work stops. An agent reads your files, performs tasks, and keeps going while you do something else. The shift isn't about changing models: it's about changing where you work.
  2. Lesson 2 · 12 minStep 1: from chat to the terminal (OSWork)The terminal is just another window. With six commands, you can move through your folders; with an AGENTS.md, the agent knows who you are and what it can do; with Git, you can undo any mistake.
  3. Lesson 3 · 10 minStep 2: your Jarvis with INTELECTOA personal assistant written from scratch in Python, kept small on purpose: it talks with you on Telegram, remembers your conversations, and uses a cloud or local model. You stop opening a website to chat: the assistant is yours.
  4. Lesson 4 · 12 minStep 3: queues — ten tasks, then go to sleepAn agent working alone does one thing at a time, and only while you’re watching. With a durable queue, each request becomes a saved task that can resume if something goes down, and workflows stop at a gate before anything important happens.
  5. Lesson 5 · 14 minStep 4: social media on autopilot, with sources on screenr7e builds the reel in seven trigger steps. promoavatar makes avatar videos for each audience. The xviral agents watch what the biggest names in the niche have posted, check every number, and publish with the source on screen. The engine is the same: only the topic changes.
  6. Lesson 6 · 10 minYour plan: one step at a timeYou’ve seen the four steps. Now make a dated plan, do the exercises in order, and move up only when “done when” is true.

Who it’s for

  1. 1People who use ChatGPT, Claude, or Gemini and want to stop copying and pasting.
  2. 2Professionals who want their own assistant and tasks running without constant supervision.
  3. 3People who will guide teams or clients through this transition and need the full roadmap.

  4. 4Follow a four-week plan with 23 verifiable exercises.
