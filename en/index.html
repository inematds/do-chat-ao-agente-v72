The lessons

An overview of each step. Each step has a complete course, linked at the end of its lesson.

  1. Lesson 1 · 6 minFrom chat to agent: the four stepsIn chat, you drive the whole time, and when you close the window, the work stops. The agent reads your files, runs tasks, and keeps going while you do something else. The shift isn't switching models: it's changing where the work happens.
  2. Lesson 2 · 12 minStep 1: from chat to terminal (OSWork)The terminal is just another window. With six commands you can walk through your folders; with an AGENTS.md the agent knows who you are and what it may do; with Git you can undo any mistake.
  3. Lesson 3 · 10 minStep 2: your Jarvis with INTELECTOA personal assistant written from scratch in Python, small on purpose: it talks to you on Telegram, remembers your conversations and uses a model in the cloud or local. You stop opening a website to chat: the assistant is yours.
  4. Lesson 4 · 12 minStep 3: queues — ten tasks and go to sleepA single agent does one thing at a time, and only while you're watching. With a durable queue, each request becomes a saved task, resumed if it crashes, and the flows stop at a gate before anything that matters.
  5. Lesson 5 · 14 minStep 4: social media on autopilot, with the source on screenr7e builds the reel in seven trigger-based stages. promoavatar makes avatar videos for each audience. The xviral tools watch what the biggest channels in the niche have published, check every number, and publish with the source on screen. The engine is the same: only the topic changes.
  6. Lesson 6 · 10 minYour plan: one step at a timeYou've now seen all four steps. Now it's time to build the plan with dates, do the exercises in order, and only move up when the "done when" is true.

Who it's for

  1. 1People who use ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and want to stop copying and pasting.
  2. 2Professionals who want an assistant of their own and tasks running without constant supervision.
  3. 3People who will guide teams or clients through this transition and need the full map.
