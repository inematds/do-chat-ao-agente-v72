Lesson 5 · 14 min

Step 4: social media on autopilot, with the source on screen

r7e builds the reel in seven trigger-based stages. promoavatar makes avatar videos for each audience. The xviral tools watch what the biggest channels in the niche have published, check every number, and publish with the source on screen. The engine is the same: only the topic changes.

  1. 1/6

    References

    The biggest channels in your niche and the rules: what counts as a fact, the required notice, and what you never publish.

  2. 2/6

    Base with source

    From the transcript, each number goes in with who said it, the video, and the literal excerpt. You check each excerpt against the transcript.

  3. 3/6

    Script

    The agent writes the script using only what is in the base. A number that isn't in the base? The script goes back.

  4. 4/6

    Gate

    Before publishing, you approve. Each rejection becomes a new rule in regras.md.

  5. 5/6

    Schedule

    You decide how often (e.g., Monday to Friday in the early morning and a long one on Saturday). A day with no topic doesn't become a video, and a failure alerts you on Telegram.

  6. 6/6

    The whole pipeline

    References and rules feed the base with a source; the base limits the script; the gate decides what goes out; the schedule posts only on days with a topic. Every refusal becomes a new rule.

Without a queue and a gate, automating social media just means posting the wrong thing faster.

02 · The reel in 7 stages (r7e)

Click each step. Illustrative example for the real estate niche.

1. Hook

The first sentence already states the gain. No "Imagine that...".

ExampleYou may be paying rent above the going rate for your area.

2. Desire

Show what changes in the life of the person watching.

ExamplePay a fair price and use the difference on what matters.

3. Fear

The cost of doing nothing, without exaggeration.

ExampleEvery month you don't check is money you don't get back.

4. Ease

Show that the first step is small.

ExampleIt takes five minutes and all you need is the address.

5. Discovery

This is where the name comes in: the tool, the method, the course.

ExampleUse the price table by neighborhood that I left in the link.

6. Benefit

The result the person takes away, again and more concrete.

ExampleYou get to the contract renewal with the number in hand.

7. Comment

The final invitation, with just one word.

ExampleComment RENT and I'll send you the table.

04 · Practice: the 6 exercises of sequence 4

Prerequisite: sequences 1, 2, and 3.

E1 · A reel in 7 steps, on paper

20 min

Write a 30 to 45 second reel for your niche using the r7e model.

Done when: you can read it aloud in under 45 s and the first sentence already states the benefit.

E2 · The 5 for your niche

20 min

Pick 5 reference channels and write the rules for your niche.

Done when: you have canais.md and regras.md for your niche.

E3 · The base with a source

30 min

Transcribe a reference video and build base.md with the number, who said it, the video and the exact excerpt.

Done when: each line has the exact excerpt and you found each one in the transcript.

E4 · A 60 s script with the source on screen

30 min

Ask the agent for a script based on the base: the strongest number, three facts with who said them, a warning and an invitation.

Done when: every number in the script is in base.md.

E5 · The schedule

20 min

Decide how often to post and write the schedule (cron or systemd timer).

Done when: the schedule is written and you know what happens on a day with no topic.

E6 · The gate before publishing

1 week

Run for one week with manual approval and write down each rejection as a rule.

Done when: you went 3 days in a row without having to reject anything.

Full exercises and the extra challenge (3 promoavatar videos for the same audience): jornada-agentes/exercicios/04-redes.md.
