Step 4: social media on autopilot, with sources on screen

r7e builds the reel in seven trigger steps. promoavatar makes avatar videos for each audience. The xviral agents watch what the biggest names in the niche have posted, check every number, and publish with the source on screen. The engine is the same: only the topic changes.

  1. 1/6

    References

    The biggest channels in your niche and the rules: what counts as a fact, the required disclaimer, and what you never publish.

  2. 2/6

    Sourced database

    Using the transcript, each number is recorded with who said it, the video, and the exact quote. You check each passage in the transcript.

  3. 3/6

    Script

    The agent writes the script using only what’s in the database. A number outside the database? The script goes back.

  4. 4/6

    Gate

    You approve before publishing. Each rejection becomes a new rule in regras.md.

  5. 5/6

    Schedule

    You decide the frequency (e.g., Monday through Friday before dawn, and one long video on Saturday). A day without a topic doesn’t become a video, and failures trigger a Telegram alert.

  6. 6/6

    The whole workflow

    References and rules feed the sourced database; the database limits the script; the gate decides what goes out; the schedule publishes only on days with a topic. Each rejection becomes a new rule.

Without a queue and a gate, automating social media means posting the wrong thing faster.

02 · The reel in 7 steps (r7e)

Click each step. Illustrative example for the real estate niche.

1. Hook

The first sentence already states the payoff. No “Imagine...”

ExampleYou may be paying more rent than the going rate in your area.

2. Desire

Show what changes in the viewer’s life.

ExamplePay a fair price and use the difference for what matters.

3. Fear

The cost of doing nothing, without exaggeration.

ExampleEvery month you don’t check is money you won’t get back.

4. Ease

Show that the first step is small.

ExampleIt takes five minutes, and all you need is the address.

5. Discovery

This is where you name the tool, method, or course.

ExampleUse the neighborhood price table I linked.

6. Benefit

The result the person gets, stated again and more concretely.

ExampleYou’ll go into the contract renewal with the number in hand.

7. Comment

The final invitation, using just one word.

ExampleComment RENT and I’ll send you the table.

04 · Practice: the 6 exercises in sequence 4

Prerequisite: sequences 1, 2, and 3.

E1 · A 7-step reel, on paper

20 min

Write a 30- to 45-second reel for your niche using the r7e template.

Done when: you can read it aloud in under 45 seconds, and the first sentence already states the payoff.

E2 · Your niche’s 5

20 min

Choose 5 reference channels and write your niche rules.

Done when: you have a canais.md and regras.md for your niche.

E3 · The source-backed base

30 min

Transcribe a reference video and build base.md with the number, who said it, the video, and the exact quote.

Done when: Each line has the exact quote, and you found each one in the transcript.

E4 · A 60-second script with sources on screen

30 min

Ask the agent for a script based on the base: strongest number, three facts with who said them, a disclaimer, and an invitation.

Done when: Every number in the script is in base.md.

E5 · The schedule

20 min

Choose the frequency and write the schedule (cron or systemd timer).

Done when: The schedule is written, and you know what happens on a day with no topic.

E6 · The gate before publishing

1 week

Run for a week with manual approval and record each rejection as a rule.

Done when: You went 3 days in a row without needing to reject anything.

Completed exercises and the extra challenge (3 promoavatar videos for the same audience): jornada-agentes/exercicios/04-redes.md.
