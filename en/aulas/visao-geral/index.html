Lesson 1 · 6 min

From chat to agent: the four steps

In chat, you drive the whole time, and when you close the window, the work stops. The agent reads your files, runs tasks, and keeps going while you do something else. The shift isn't switching models: it's changing where the work happens.

  1. 1/5

    1 · Terminal

    The agent (Claude Code or Codex) works inside a folder of yours, reads a rulebook (AGENTS.md), and you undo any mistake with Git. It's the foundation for everything that comes next.

  2. 2/5

    2 · Assistant

    With INTELECTO, an assistant of your own on Telegram, with memory and the style you define, using a cloud or local model.

  3. 3/5

    3 · Queues

    inemaccbot saves each request as a task, resumes from where it stopped, and halts at a gate when it needs your approval.

  4. 4/5

    4 · Networks

    r7e, promoavatar, and the xviral channels (finviral, saudeviral, advviral) publishing regularly, with checked numbers and the source on screen.

  5. 5/5

    All four together

    Each step reuses the one before: the terminal's folder and rules, the .env with the keys, the queue that holds the tasks, and the gate before what matters. That's why the order isn't optional.

No miracles: there's no "render while you sleep" if the system doesn't run correctly.

02 · Find your starting point

Click the situation that looks most like yours today.

1. I only use chat

Start at step 1. Do the OSWork Quick and then right away the six exercises of sequence 1.

Goal for the first week: get to your folder using only the terminal and watch the agent create a file inside it.

First stepOpen the terminal and create the folder projetos/meu-primeiro-agente.

2. I already use the terminal

You're already at step 1. Check that you have AGENTS.md, Git, and .env in .gitignore; if you do, move up to step 2.

Install your own Jarvis with INTELECTO and take its course.

First stepWrite the meu-jarvis.md before installing anything.

3. I already have an assistant

Time for the queue. Before installing inemaccbot, run the homemade queue in 30 lines to understand entrada, feito, and falhou.

Then list 10 repetitive tasks and mark where you need a gate.

First stepFill in the table of 10 tasks, with at least two of them having a gate.

4. I already have a queue

Step 4. Use r7e for the format and finviral as the template for your niche's channel.

Write the niche rules before automating: with no queue and no gate, automating social media just means posting the wrong thing faster.

First stepPick 5 reference channels and write canais.md and regras.md.
