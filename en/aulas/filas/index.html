Lesson 4 · 12 min

Step 3: queues — ten tasks and go to sleep

A single agent does one thing at a time, and only while you're watching. With a durable queue, each request becomes a saved task, resumed if it crashes, and the flows stop at a gate before anything that matters.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1How a durable queue works
  2. 2Resume from where it stopped
  3. 3Where to put the human gate
  4. 4Measure how many came out right

Goal: decide what goes into the queue, where the gates are, and how to check the result the next day.

01 · The path of a task

From request to result, with resume and gate.

Scroll the page: each step below lights up its part of the diagram.

  1. 1/5

    Requests

    Each request becomes a task: in inemaccbot, a message on Telegram; in the homemade queue, a .md file in the entrada/ folder.

  2. 2/5

    Durable queue

    The task stays saved. If the computer shuts down or you interrupt it, the next round picks up what is left, without redoing what was already done.

  3. 3/5

    Agent

    The agent runs one task at a time. If it works, the answer goes to feito; if it errors, it goes to falhou, with the reason, for you to look at.

  4. 4/5

    Gate

    In the flows that matter, the bot writes and stops. You read, fix it if needed, and only then approve. This is the moment you manage, instead of doing the work.

  5. 5/5

    One night of queue

    You send the tasks and go to sleep. The queue saves them, the agent runs one at a time and stops at the gate for what matters. In the morning, you look at feito/ and falhou/.

Examples of flows that become tasks in inemaccbot: transcribe, dub, explainer video, course and reel.

04 · Practice: the 5 exercises of sequence 3

Prerequisite: sequences 1 and 2.

E1 · The queue on paper

15 min

List 10 repetitive tasks and mark whether they run on their own, whether they need a gate, and where.

Done when: all 10 lines are filled in and at least two have a gate.

E2 · A homemade queue in 30 lines

30 min

Create two tasks in fila/entrada and run fila-caseira.sh. Interrupt one run with Ctrl+C.

Done when: both are in fila/feito and you saw the queue pick up where it left off.

E3 · Install inemaccbot

1 h

Follow the guide until the bot responds on Telegram. Then test /ajuda and /fila.

Done when: /ping responds in your Telegram.

E4 · Ten tasks and go to sleep

1 night

Send 5 to 10 tasks from your list and go do something else.

Done when: the next day you checked each result and noted how many came out right on the first try.

E5 · The gate

30 min

Use a flow with a gate, like /promoavatar: read, correct, and only then approve.

Done when: you rejected or corrected at least one text before approving.

Full exercises and the extra challenge (a new rule for the task that failed the most): jornada-agentes/exercicios/03-filas.md.
