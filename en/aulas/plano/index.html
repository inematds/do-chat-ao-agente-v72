    Terminal

    The foundation for everything. Six exercises take you from your first mkdir to an agent reading your files and citing its sources. Without this step in place, the other three get stuck.

  2. 2/5

    Your Jarvis

    Requires step 1 (terminal, folders, and .env). From the plan in meu-jarvis.md to a routine used five days in a row; the memory test takes two days.

  3. 3/5

    Queues

    Requires steps 1 and 2. The queue on paper and the homemade queue come before the bot; a night of tasks shows how many finish correctly on the first try.

  4. 4/5

    Social media

    Requires the previous three steps: without a queue and a gate, automating social media means posting the wrong thing faster. It ends with a week of manual approval.

  5. 5/5

    The full training

    Four steps in order, each with its own exercise list and a verifiable criterion. Move up only when “done when” for the previous step is true.

Required order: each step builds on what the previous one set up.
